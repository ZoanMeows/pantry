Classic Butter Chicken

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4.8/5 based on 124 ratings)

Ready in 45 mins

Prep Time: 45 minutes
Origin: Indian
Servings: 4 people
Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients

  • 500g Boneless Chicken (diced)
  • 2 tbsp Butter
  • 1 cup Tomato Puree
  • 1/2 cup Heavy Cream
  • 1 tbsp Garam Masala
  • 1 tsp Red Chilli Powder
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Marinate: Mix chicken pieces with yogurt, a pinch of salt, and spices. Let sit for 30 mins.
  2. Cook the chicken: Pan-fry the marinated chicken until browned on all sides. Set aside.
  3. Prepare the gravy: Add butter and tomato puree to the pan. Cook until oil starts to separate.
  4. Combine: Add the cooked chicken, heavy cream, and garam masala to the tomato gravy. Simmer for 5-7 minutes.
  5. Serve: Garnish with fresh cream and serve hot.
